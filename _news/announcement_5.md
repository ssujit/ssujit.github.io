The Geolinking Service SoRa, developed by IOER, GESIS, and SOEP/DIW Berlin, helps researchers link social science survey data with spatial data. It supports the workflow from selecting data through linkage to analysis, with privacy safeguards: actual SOEP residential coordinates are available only in the secure FDZ SOEP environment.

[Read the announcement](https://nachrichten.idw-online.de/2026/11/04/linking-data-new-leibniz-infrastructure-strengthens-interdisciplinary-research?groupcolor=2) or visit the [Geolinking Service SoRa](https://sora-service.org/en/).
